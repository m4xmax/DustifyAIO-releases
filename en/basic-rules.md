# Basic rules

These rules apply to every Dustify member and DustifyAIO user. Breaking them can result in an **immediate ban** from Dustify and, in serious cases, **legal action**.

## What is forbidden

### Leaking

- Sharing builds, installers, update links, or internal files outside official channels
- Posting screenshots with sensitive data (licenses, webhooks, tokens, accounts) to public / third-party servers
- Copying parts of the bot, modules, configs, or docs outside Dustify

### Abuse and bypassing protection

- Attempts to crack, patch, reverse engineer, decompile, or modify the bot
- Bypassing Discord auth, role checks, or license / membership validation
- Using the bot after membership expires or is revoked
- Tampering with updates, telemetry, or security checks

### Sharing licenses / access

- Lending, selling, or sharing access with anyone who does not have their own active membership
- Sharing one Discord account / one license across multiple people
- Giving Dustify server access to people who are not entitled to it

### Attacks and harmful behavior

- Any attacks against DustifyAIO, infrastructure, Discord, APIs, or other members
- Spam, webhook abuse, flooding support, phishing the community
- Spreading malware or fake “Dustify” builds

### Other

- Reselling the bot or Dustify services without explicit permission
- Public scripts / tutorials that help bypass protection or these rules

## Consequences

| Severity | Typical outcome |
|----------|-----------------|
| Minor violation | Warning, temporary ban |
| Repeated / intentional | Permanent Dustify ban |
| Leak, crack, attack, selling access | Immediate ban + possible legal action |

Dustify reserves the right to decide sanctions based on the situation. Not knowing the rules is not an excuse.

## Fair use

- Use the bot only for yourself and within your membership
- Download only from official Discord links
- Report suspected leaks or scam builds to staff / a ticket immediately
