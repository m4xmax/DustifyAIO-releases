# Stats

**Stats** is the overview of your checkouts.

## Features

- filter checkouts
- switch **currency**
- delete individual orders (e.g. after a cancel)

## Success posts

Don’t forget to post nice **success screenshots** to success channels.

That helps the community and staff see real results, and gives you a clean archive.

## Tip

After a cancel, delete the order in Stats so your numbers stay honest.
