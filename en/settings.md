# Settings

## Webhooks

In Settings, configure your **Discord webhooks** and **test** them.

**We recommend webhooks for everyone.** They send important info, for example:

- payment details / links so you can **pay directly from the webhook**
- **order numbers** in one clear place
- success / error visibility without living only inside the app

Without webhooks it’s easy to miss a payment window or order details.

> Don’t share webhook URLs publicly — anyone with the URL can spam your channel.

## Global delays

In Settings you can set **global delays**.

If you don’t know what you’re doing, don’t change anything. Bad delays can hurt success rate or increase errors.
